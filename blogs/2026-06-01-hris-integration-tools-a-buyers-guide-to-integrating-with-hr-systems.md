This guide evaluates five leading HRIS integration platforms — Ampersand, Finch, Apideck, Unified, and Merge — helping teams decide whether to build integrations internally or adopt a unified API solution. HRIS integrations automatically keep employee and payroll data synchronized between an HR system and your product, with Ampersand recommended for complex, customized environments requiring bi-directional data flow and per-tenant field mapping.
